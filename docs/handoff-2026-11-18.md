## 5. Contracts other code depends on

**Chat DOM contract** — three probes and the e2e specs navigate by it; changing any
part is a deliberate, cross-file edit:
`#question`, the `Send` button, `#answer-status` markers (`Answer ready`,
`Step 4 is ready`), `#chat-history article:last` with `:scope > p` paragraphs,
`button[data-chat-choice]` carrying `data-choice-type` ∈ `town|region|farm|follow-up`
inside a `role="group"` with a non-empty `aria-label`, `#latitude`, `#longitude`, and
`window.fieldshiftAnswerLatencyMs` stamped per answer.

**Country names are runtime-dependent.** Step-1 `<select id="pest-country">` labels come
from the browser's `Intl.DisplayNames`; recognition in
[public/js/lib/countries.js](../public/js/lib/countries.js) merges `en-001`, splits
parenthetical spellings, and adds a hand-maintained `aliases` list for the spellings
that exist only in one engine (e.g. `hong kong sar china`, `macao sar china`,
`palestinian territories`). `npm run test:country-spelling` proves parity; the offline
test asserts the alias list itself, because a runtime that resolves the name from its
own table cannot see the gap.

**Place data is source-faithful, never cleaned.** The saved GeoNames extract has 245
lists for 250 supported codes; 18 lists have unnamed regions, 7 hold a town spelled like
the country, 11 hold a town spelled like a *different* region, and 5 codes (AQ BV HM IO
UM) have no list at all. Fixes belong in the interaction layer, not in the data.

**Field lookup budgets** ([server/context/farms.js](../server/context/farms.js),
[public/js/modules/workflow-assistant.js](../public/js/modules/workflow-assistant.js)):
an unreachable service is reported fast (no retry ladder), a busy or slow one keeps the
hedge ladder up to the 75 s shared deadline, and the **guided chat** waits only
`fieldsGraceMs` (10 s) before answering with the town point plus the two real way-ons.
Step 1's own button keeps the full deadline. Do not "optimise" the ladder away: the
tests pin that all three services are still attempted.

**Reply composition budget** ([public/js/lib/chat-context.js](../public/js/lib/chat-context.js)):
`contextBlocks()` keeps at most two *measured* blocks plus one `limitation` block, and
`composeAnswerBlocks()` lets context take all three panel paragraphs only when one of them is a NASA or
SoilGrids estimate; descriptive context (field record, pest occurrence, national ratio) keeps two and
leaves the third for the answer's own crop paragraph. The split exists because a single shared budget of
two silently deleted the ISRIC "not a farm measurement" advisory from a weather-plus-soil reply
(validation.md subsection 22); `tests/chat-context.test.js` pins both halves. The pest paragraph picks its
record in a fixed order — an insect named in the question, then the watchlist entry whose `cropIds`
intersect `state.planning.baseline`/`allowedCrops`, then the first entry — because the shipped table has two
entries and list order is not relevance (subsection 23); that test builds its fixture from the real
`PESTS` export in `server/context/pests.js`, so adding a watchlist entry changes the guard's input too. "Named
in the question" means the words that identify a species, matched as whole words with an optional plural: the
head noun when it is long enough to carry the name (`armyworm`, `locust`) and, when the head is short and
generic (`moth`), the modifier in front of it as well — never every word, because a match on every word is how
the season word `fall` or the demonym `Australian` would open a pest paragraph (subsection 24).

**Citation ids belong to the paragraph, not the record**: every `sources.push` in `chat-context.js` writes its
`id` **after** the spread. `chat.js:108` counts the ids the rendered paragraphs cite and `dom.js:16-17` skips one
it cannot resolve, so a record that brings its own id renames the citation and leaves a `Sources (n)` header with
fewer links under it (subsection 25). `tests/chat-context.test.js` asserts the pairing for the shipped shapes and
for records that disagree with them.

**Tiny word LM.** `public/models/fieldshift-mini` is pinned by sha256 in
`tests/model.test.js`. Rebuild/retrain it only when the corpus vocabulary or a reply
label actually changes — `scripts/build-mini-corpus.js` reads
`models/mini-development-questions.json` and `tests/qa/prompts.json` only, so place-data,
country-table and chat-prose changes do **not** require a retrain.

## 6. Invariants that were paid for with real defects

1. No dead control: every offered button resolves to a real saved row; a reply may not
   offer a button back to the same question.
2. Unnamed regions are never asked for; the area prompt offers the first three real
   towns of the visible step-1 list and labels that as "not a recommendation".
3. A reply equal to a country's whole spelling may only select a row whose own whole
   spelling equals it (the São Tomé class of bug), and the country's own name answered at
   a place question gets an honest reply ending "Nothing was selected".
4. A town spelled like a region resolves to the **town row** (inside the chosen region
   first, then anywhere in the country list); two towns sharing name and region get
   coordinate-suffixed labels; truncation says "showing 3 of N".
5. An unmatched short reply inside a chosen region returns the town-recovery prompt with
   real buttons; unmatched free text still runs no action.
6. Codes with no saved list keep `Whole <country>` and explain the gap instead of
   offering zero buttons.
7. `sourceLinks` links only absolute `http(s)` URLs; no invented confidence numbers,
   yields or savings anywhere.
8. Tests are widened, never weakened or skipped. If a test asserted a superseded
   behaviour, rewrite the assertion to the new required behaviour and say so.
