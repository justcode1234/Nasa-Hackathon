# Optional 30-second live narration

FieldShift turns NASA weather evidence into crop-rotation comparisons. Search for
a place and choose a mapped field. Inspect historical temperature, rain and solar
data with sources, dates and gaps. Select your crops to compare your baseline with
two alternatives over the same year. See family repeats, calendar fit and known
thermal warnings. Country pest records and an optional local assistant add context.
Every result makes limits visible: this is decision support for local review,
not a guarantee of farming outcomes.

This is a narration starting point, not a rendered or time-verified video. The
submission-ready demo artifact is the seven-slide deck, so a video is not required.
