Search a town/region/country, then choose a field outline or dropdown. OpenStreetMap crop tags are shown when recorded. Missing crop histories remain unknown.

Choose crops without typing

Country dropdowns use 9,238 FAOSTAT production records across 197 countries/areas. Only recorded categories with starter rules form alternatives; missing coverage remains unknown.

Use existing evidence

SoilGrids supplies 250 m regional pH, sand/clay and model quantiles. ABS supplies dated Australian irrigation totals and documented annual ML/ha ratios.

Neither becomes a measured farm soil test or an irrigation prescription. Country pest records are inspection context.

Mapped land is not verified ownership, access or current production. ISRIC advises against farm-level use; national watering averages are not schedules.

Custom next-word training completed in browser TensorFlow.js/WASM. Evidence renders before inference. A lexical/relevance guard can reject the model selection. Raw generated scientific claims never reach the UI.

Not a general-purpose LLM. Development results: 75/90 selections; only 9/18 free-form cases. Wording/splits are limited. These are not agronomic accuracy or confidence figures.
