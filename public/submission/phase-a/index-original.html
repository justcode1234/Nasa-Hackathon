Choose a country, region and town. Search and map input cover missing places. OSM field outlines can lack crop tags.

Know the coverage

9,238 FAOSTAT records cover 197 countries/areas; only 89 have enough screening crops to compare. Radish has no FAOSTAT category and is never offered by country lists.

Use existing evidence

SoilGrids supplies 250 m regional pH, sand/clay and model quantiles. ABS supplies dated Australian irrigation totals and annual ML/ha ratios.

Neither is a farm soil test or irrigation prescription. Country pest records are inspection context.

Mapped land is not verified ownership, access or current production. ISRIC advises against farm-level use; national watering averages are not schedules.

Custom next-word training completed in browser TensorFlow.js/WASM. Evidence renders before inference. A relevance guard allows prioritizing existing retrieved paragraphs; all text and caveats remain. Raw generated scientific claims never reach the UI. Fixed app actions also guide country/field selection, farmer inputs, NASA retrieval and comparison through step 4.

Not a general-purpose LLM. Development results: 85/126 selections; 14/36 new free-form matches versus 8/36 before. Wording/splits are limited. These are not agronomic accuracy or confidence figures.
