FieldShift assistant

Evidence is available immediately. The compact model loads when chat opens.

Compact local language-model experiment

FieldShift Mini is a custom next-word language-model experiment, plus a small TensorFlow.js runtime. Its measured weight size appears after loading. It is not a general-purpose LLM. Development tests showed limited free-form understanding, so a relevance guard and sourced fallback are always used. Raw generated scientific claims are never displayed.

Ask about a crop and country, such as “rice in India”. Or ask “help me plan a rotation in India” to select a field, enter your three crops, load NASA evidence and compare in step 4.
